Community Guidelines

Effective: 8 November 2026, 6:00 PM IST  ·  Version 1.0.0

Opinio is a space for real people to share honest opinions and engage in meaningful conversations. We want everyone — regardless of background, belief, or viewpoint — to feel safe participating. These guidelines apply to every post, comment, profile, community, and direct message on Opinio, whether posted publicly or under an anonymous persona.

What is not allowed

  • Child sexual abuse material or the sexualisation of minors. This is a zero-tolerance offence. We report such content to the authorities and permanently close the account without warning.
  • Threats, incitement to violence, and terrorism. Content that threatens individuals or groups, encourages harm to people or animals, or glorifies acts of terror is strictly prohibited.
  • Hate speech and discrimination. We do not allow content that attacks or demeans a person or group based on religion, caste, race, ethnicity, gender, sexual orientation, disability, nationality, or any other protected characteristic.
  • Harassment and bullying. Repeated targeting, intimidation, or abuse of any individual — whether publicly or via private messages — is not permitted.
  • Sharing private information without consent (doxxing). Posting someone's personal details — such as address, phone number, or workplace — without their permission, or sharing intimate images without consent, is prohibited.
  • Sexually explicit content and graphic violence. Pornographic material and graphic gore are not allowed on Opinio.
  • Scams, spam, and misleading engagement. Fake accounts, bot activity, paid promotions disguised as organic opinion, and selling illegal goods or services are all prohibited.
  • Impersonation. Do not pretend to be another person, public figure, government body, or organisation.
  • Dangerous misinformation. Deliberately spreading false information that could cause public harm — for example, false health advice, election fraud claims without basis, or fake emergency alerts — is not permitted.
  • Content that is illegal under Indian law. This includes content prohibited under the Information Technology Act, 2000, the Indian Penal Code, and any other applicable legislation.
  • Evading a suspension. Using an anonymous persona or a new account to bypass a suspension or to continue harassing someone from a "clean" identity is a violation of these guidelines.

Disagreement is welcome

We encourage debate and healthy disagreement. You are absolutely free to challenge ideas, question claims, and hold strong opinions. The key rule is simple: challenge the argument, not the person. Critique is fine; personal attacks are not.

If you are struggling or someone is in danger

If you or someone you know may be at risk of harming themselves or others, please reach out to emergency services or a helpline immediately. In India, you can contact iCall at 9152987821 or the Vandrevala Foundation Helpline at 1860-2662-345 (available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, free of cost).

Consequences for breaking these rules

Depending on the seriousness of the violation, we may remove the content, issue a warning, restrict your posting ability, temporarily suspend your account, or permanently ban you. See our Moderation page for details on how we review content and how you can appeal a decision.

How to report something

Use the flag or "Report" button on any post, comment, profile, persona, or chat message to bring something to our attention. You can also write to us directly at noreply.opinio@gmail.com. Every report is reviewed by a human member of our team.
