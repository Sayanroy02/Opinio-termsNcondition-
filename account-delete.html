Account Deletion

Effective: 8 November 2026, 6:00 PM IST  ·  Version 1.0.0

You have full control over your Opinio account. This page explains exactly how to delete your account, what happens to your data when you do, and how to reach us if you need help.

Important: Account deletion is permanent. After the 7-day grace period, your data cannot be recovered. Please make sure you download any content you wish to keep before you proceed.

How to delete your account

  1. Open the Opinio app on your device.
  2. Go to Settings (tap your profile picture, then the gear icon).
  3. Tap Privacy & data.
  4. Tap Delete my account.
  5. Read the confirmation screen and tap Confirm deletion.

Your account is hidden from other users immediately. You have 7 days to change your mind — during this window you can log back in and tap Restore my account to cancel the deletion. After 7 days, your account and all associated data is permanently and irreversibly deleted from our systems.

What gets deleted

When your account is permanently deleted, we remove all of the following from our systems:

  • Your account credentials — email address, password hash, and date of birth
  • Your public profile — name, handle, bio, and profile photo
  • All anonymous personas linked to your account
  • Every post, comment, vote, poll answer, and draft you created
  • Your follows, followers, and community memberships
  • All photos, videos, and files you uploaded
  • Your private chat messages from our servers (note: copies may still exist on other users' devices)
  • Your notification settings and device tokens
  • Any saved preferences or app settings stored in your account

What we keep and for how long

A small amount of data is retained after deletion, strictly as required by Indian law and for the safety of our community:

DataRetention periodReason
System backups (which may include your data)Up to 30 days after deletionDisaster recovery. This data is not restored in response to account deletion requests.
Moderation records and reports involving your accountAt least 180 days after the case is closedRequired under the IT (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021
Record that you agreed to our Terms and Privacy Policy1 year after account closureLegal record of consent
Staff audit logs (action records only — no message content)1 yearInternal accountability

Once the applicable retention period expires, this remaining data is also permanently deleted. We do not use it for any other purpose.

Can't access the app? Request deletion by email

If you are unable to log in to the app to delete your account yourself, you can request deletion by writing to us at noreply.opinio@gmail.com from the email address registered to your account. Please use the subject line "Account Deletion Request" and include your registered handle or username so we can identify your account. We will process your request within 7 business days and confirm once it is done.

Grievances and further questions

If you have any questions about account deletion or how your data is handled, please contact our Grievance Officer, Ankit Sharma, at noreply.opinio@gmail.com. We acknowledge all queries within 24 hours and aim to resolve them within 15 business days, in accordance with the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021.
