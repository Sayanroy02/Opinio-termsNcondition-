Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 Nov, 2026 · Version 1.0.0 (2026-11-08) (must match the LEGAL_VERSION setting on the server)

1. What this explains

What personal data Opinio collects, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it and what choices you have. We handle personal data under Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and its rules.

2. What we collect

DataWhy
Email address, password (stored only as a one-way hash), date of birthCreate and secure your account; enforce the 18+ rule
Profile details you add (name, handle, bio, photos)Show your public profile
Posts, comments, votes, follows, communities, drafts, poll answersRun the service and show your content
Anonymous personas and the link to your accountLet you post anonymously to others while keeping accounts safe and accountable
Pictures, videos and files you uploadShow them in posts and chats (chat files are encrypted)

Crash reportsFix bugs (sent to our error-reporting provider without request contents)
Reports you make and decisions about contentModeration and safety
Your agreement to our Terms and Privacy Policy (version and time)Record your consent

3. Why we may use it

To provide Opinio, keep it safe, follow the law and, with your consent, for the purposes listed above. You can withdraw consent by deleting your account.

4. Who we share it with

Only service providers that help us run Opinio, under contract: cloud hosting (Render), database (Neon), file storage and delivery (Cloudflare), email sending (Brevo), push notifications (Google Firebase), crash reporting (Sentry), image-safety checking (Sightengine) and an AI provider that checks claims (Anthropic; the text of Claim posts is sent to it).

We do not sell your personal data. We disclose data to authorities only as described in Legal requests.

5. How long we keep it

See Data retention.

6. Your rights

You can see and download your data (Settings → Privacy & data → Download my data), correct your profile, and delete your account. You can also write to us to exercise any right the law gives you, to nominate someone to act for you, or to complain: noreply.opinio@gmail.com.

7. Children

Opinio is only for people aged 18 and over. If we learn that someone younger has an account we will remove it.

8. Security

Passwords are hashed, data is encrypted in transit, private chats are end-to-end encrypted and access to our systems is limited and logged. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

9. Changes

If we change this policy in an important way we will ask you to review and agree again in the app.
