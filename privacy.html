Privacy Policy

Effective: 8 November 2026, 6:00 PM IST  ·  Version 1.0.0

1. Our commitment to your privacy

At Opinio, we believe that your personal data belongs to you — and that you deserve to know exactly how it is handled. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and what choices you have. We comply with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and all other applicable Indian data-protection laws.

2. What we collect and why

What we collectWhy we need it
Email address, password (stored as a secure one-way hash), and date of birthTo create and protect your account and to ensure only adults (18+) use the platform
Profile details you choose to add — name, handle, bio, and photoTo display your public profile to other users
Posts, comments, votes, follows, communities, saved drafts, and poll answersTo run the core features of the app and show your activity
Anonymous personas and their link to your accountTo allow anonymous posting while keeping the platform safe and accountable
Photos, videos, and files you uploadTo display them in posts and chats (files in chats are encrypted)
Private chat messagesDelivered using end-to-end encryption — we cannot read them
Device identifiers, push notification tokens, app version, and basic technical logs including approximate IP addressTo send you notifications, maintain security, and diagnose technical issues
Crash reportsTo identify and fix bugs (sent without the content of your posts or messages)
Reports you submit and related moderation decisionsFor safety review and platform integrity
Record of your agreement to our Terms and Privacy PolicyTo maintain a record of your consent, as required by law

3. How we use your data

We use your data only to operate Opinio, keep the platform safe, and fulfil our legal obligations under Indian law. Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time by deleting your account. We do not use your data for advertising and we never sell it to anyone.

4. Who we share your data with

We share data only with third-party service providers that help us run Opinio — and only under strict contractual obligations. These include our cloud hosting provider (Render), database provider (Neon), file storage and delivery provider (Cloudflare), email service (Brevo), push notification service (Google Firebase), crash-reporting tool (Sentry), image-safety scanning tool (Sightengine), and an AI fact-checking provider (Anthropic — the text of Claim-type posts is sent to them for analysis).

We do not sell your personal data. We only share data with government authorities or law-enforcement agencies as described in our Legal Requests policy.

5. How long we keep your data

See our Data Retention page for a full breakdown of retention periods for each type of data.

6. Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and other applicable Indian laws, you have the right to access, correct, and erase your personal data. You can download a copy of your data or delete your account directly in the app under Settings → Privacy & data. You may also write to us to exercise any right, to appoint a nominee to act on your behalf, or to raise a concern: noreply.opinio@gmail.com.

7. Children and minors

Opinio is strictly for people aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If we discover that a minor has created an account, we will delete it promptly. If you believe a minor is using our platform, please report it to us immediately.

8. How we protect your data

We take security seriously. Passwords are stored as one-way hashes and are never readable. All data in transit is encrypted using industry-standard protocols. Private chats are end-to-end encrypted. Access to our internal systems is restricted to authorised personnel and every access is logged. If a data breach occurs that could affect you, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required under Indian law.

9. Changes to this policy

If we make a significant change to this Privacy Policy, we will notify you inside the app and ask you to review and accept the updated version. We will not silently change how we use your data.
