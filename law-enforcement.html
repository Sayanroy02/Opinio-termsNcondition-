Legal Requests

Effective: 8 November 2026, 6:00 PM IST  ·  Version 1.0.0

Opinio is committed to protecting the privacy of all its users, including those who post under anonymous personas. We take every legal request seriously and evaluate each one carefully before taking any action. This page explains how we handle requests from law-enforcement agencies, courts, and other government authorities in India.

What a valid request must include

We require all legal requests to meet the following standards before we act on them:

  • A formal written request or court order from a duly authorised authority under Indian law, clearly citing the specific legal provision or section being relied upon.
  • A precise description of the account, anonymous persona, post, or time period in question. We do not comply with overly broad or bulk requests that go beyond what is legally necessary.
  • Official contact details of the requesting officer or authority so that we can verify the authenticity of the request.

What we can and cannot provide

  • We cannot provide the content of private chat messages. All direct messages on Opinio are end-to-end encrypted and are not accessible to us.
  • We can identify which registered account is associated with an anonymous persona. However, we will only disclose this information in response to a valid court order or direction under applicable Indian law, after review by our authorised legal representative, Ankit Sharma.
  • Unless the court order or applicable law expressly prohibits us from doing so, we will notify the user whose data has been requested, giving them an opportunity to respond or seek legal remedy.

Emergency disclosures

If we have a genuine, credible belief that someone's life is in immediate danger, we may — at our sole discretion — share limited information with the relevant authority without waiting for a formal order. Any such disclosure will be documented internally, including the reason and the specific data shared.

Requests from outside India

Opinio is an Indian company operating under Indian law. We do not respond to requests from foreign governments or agencies unless they are routed through the appropriate Mutual Legal Assistance Treaty (MLAT) process or are otherwise mandated under applicable Indian legislation.

How to send a legal request

All legal requests must be sent in writing to: noreply.opinio@gmail.com
Please include all required information listed above. Incomplete requests will not be processed.
