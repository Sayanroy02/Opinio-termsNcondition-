Legal requests

Effective date: 8 Nov, 2026 · Version 1.0.0 (2026-11-08) (must match the LEGAL_VERSION setting on the server)

We take privacy seriously, including the privacy of anonymous personas. This page explains how we respond to requests from authorities.

What we need

  • A written request or order from an authority that is legally entitled to ask, citing the law it relies on.
  • The specific account, persona, post or time period concerned. We push back on requests that are too broad.
  • Contact details of the official so we can verify the request.

What we can and cannot provide

  • We cannot read private chats (end-to-end encrypted).
  • We can see which account owns an anonymous persona. We disclose that link only in response to a valid legal order, after review by our authorised legal representative, Ankit Sharma.
  • We tell the affected person about a request unless the law or the order forbids it.

Emergencies

If someone's life is at immediate risk we may share limited information without a formal order and will record why.
