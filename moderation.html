How We Moderate

Effective: 8 November 2026, 6:00 PM IST  ·  Version 1.0.0

We want Opinio to be a place where people feel comfortable speaking freely. Our moderation process is designed to be fair, transparent, and consistent. Here is how it works.

Reporting content

Anyone on Opinio can report a post, comment, profile, anonymous persona, or chat message that they believe violates our Community Guidelines. When you submit a report, a case is created and reviewed by our team. The person who reports a post is never publicly identified, and the account being reported is not told who filed the report. If the content is from an anonymous persona, the identity behind that persona is never disclosed to the reporter.

How we review reports

Every report is looked at by a human reviewer. We also use automated tools to help detect obvious violations faster — such as basic content filters, upload screening for explicit imagery, and an automated fact-checking system for Claim-type posts. However, automated tools only assist our team; they never make final decisions on their own. A real person always reviews before any action is taken.

Private chats

All private messages on Opinio are end-to-end encrypted. This means we cannot read them, scan them, or access them in any way. If you report a chat message, you are actively choosing to share that message with our moderation team for review — this is the only circumstance under which we can see private messages.

What actions we can take

Depending on the seriousness of a violation, our reviewers may dismiss the report (if no violation is found), remove the specific content, issue a warning to the account, restrict certain account features, temporarily suspend the account, or permanently close it. All moderation actions taken by our staff are logged in a secure audit trail.

How quickly we act

We acknowledge reports within 24 hours and aim to reach a decision within 7 business days. Reports involving immediate safety risks — such as threats of violence or self-harm — are prioritised and handled as quickly as possible. These timelines are consistent with the requirements under the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021.

Appealing a decision

If you believe we made the wrong call on your content or your account, you have the right to appeal. Write to us at noreply.opinio@gmail.com with your registered email address and a brief description of what happened. Your appeal will be reviewed by a different member of our team — not the person who made the original decision.
