Data Retention

Effective: 8 November 2026, 6:00 PM IST  ·  Version 1.0.0

We only keep your data for as long as genuinely necessary — to operate the service, to comply with Indian law, and to protect the safety of our community. The table below sets out exactly how long we hold each type of data and why.

Type of dataHow long we keep itReason
Account, profile, anonymous personas, posts, comments, votes, follows, and saved draftsUntil you delete your account, plus a 7-day grace period (you can restore your account during this window). After 7 days your data is permanently and irreversibly deleted.To run the core features of the service and to give you a chance to change your mind.
Photos, videos, and files you uploadedDeleted when the content or account is removedSame as above
Private chat messages (end-to-end encrypted)Until you or the other person deletes the conversation, the disappearing-message timer expires, or one of the accounts is deletedTo deliver messages as intended
System backupsUp to 30 days after the original data is deletedFor disaster recovery only. Data deleted by a user is not restored from a backup following a recovery event.
Server and technical logs (including approximate IP address)30 daysTo maintain security and diagnose technical faults
Reports, moderation decisions, and records related to removed contentAt least 180 days after the case is closed, as required by the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021For safety review, appeals, and legal compliance
Record of your agreement to our Terms and Privacy PolicyFor the lifetime of your account and for 1 year after it is closedTo maintain a valid record of your consent
Staff audit logs (which record actions taken by our team, but not message content)1 yearFor internal accountability and compliance

Where Indian law requires us to retain data for a period longer than those listed above, we will retain only the minimum data required, for no longer than legally necessary, and will then delete it.
